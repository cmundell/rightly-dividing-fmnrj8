1.6.0Listen: the Bible read aloud November 7, 20262 new1 changed
  • New
    Listen. Every chapter heading has a Listen button. The chapter is read aloud by George, a deep British voice, while the verse being read is marked and kept in view; reading carries on into the next chapter. A small player at the bottom has play/pause, previous and next chapter, and the reading speed (0.8x to 1.5x), which is remembered.
  • New
    Chapters are downloaded the first time they are heard and kept on the device, so they play again without a connection. Settings has a new Listening section showing what is kept; Windows can download the whole Bible at once (about 1.3 GB) for listening fully offline.
  • Credits
    The voice is George from Kokoro, an open-source speech engine (Apache-2.0), so the audio is free to keep and share like the text.
  • Changed
    The New Testament is available first; Old Testament chapters appear over the following days and say “Not recorded yet” until then.
