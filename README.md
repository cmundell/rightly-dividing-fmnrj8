# Search the Scriptures (web edition)

An offline King James Bible study app with the Treasury of Scripture Knowledge, word study, maps, family devotions and discipleship paths.

Open https://cmundell.github.io/search-the-scriptures/ on a phone and choose **Add to Home Screen**. After the first visit it works offline. Notes are kept only on the device.
